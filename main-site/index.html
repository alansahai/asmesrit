Who We Are

About ASME SRIT

A formal engineering chapter under ASME, driving technical excellence and professional growth at SRIT since 2019.

Our Vision

To be the premier student engineering organisation at SRIT — fostering innovation, leadership, and professional development through cutting-edge programs and initiatives.

Our Mission

To provide SRIT students with opportunities for technical growth, networking, and hands-on experience through ASME programs, competitions, and events that bridge academia with industry.
