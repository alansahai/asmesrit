Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

The SDG Innovation Expo is open to all engineering students across India. The Internal Hackathon is exclusively for students enrolled at Sri Ramakrishna Institute of Technology (SRIT), Coimbatore.

Teams can have 2–4 members for the Expo track. The Internal Hackathon requires exactly 3 members per team. Individuals may also participate in the Expo track.

A working prototype is strongly encouraged and will be evaluated positively. However, a well-developed conceptual model or simulation is also accepted, as long as it clearly addresses a chosen SDG theme.

Please check the official KonfHub registration portal for the current registration fee details. ASME student members may be eligible for a discounted registration.

Projects are evaluated on: SDG Alignment & Impact (30%), Technical Feasibility (25%), Innovation & Creativity (25%), and Presentation Quality (20%). An expert panel of industry professionals and faculty will serve as judges.

Yes. All participating teams will receive a certificate of participation. Winners will receive certificates of merit along with their prizes from ASME TN Section and ASME SRIT.

ASME TN-NOVATE is the flagship state-level SDG Innovation Expo organized by the ASME Student Section at Sri Ramakrishna Institute of Technology, Coimbatore.
